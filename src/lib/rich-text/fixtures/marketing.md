## Details/FAQ

**Q: Am I paid during this?**

**A:** Yes! If you fill out a daily time sheet record it as down time and clock out at the end as normal.

**Q: When is it?**

**A:** We will meet quarterly on the 2nd Wednesday of the quarter at 2:15PM ( sharp ). The first meeting will be on 10/22/25, the second on 1/14/26 and so on.

**Q: How long will it take?**

**A:** It should take 30 minutes approximately.

**Q: Where does it take place?**

**A:** Meet in the conference room in the front office.

**Q: Who's invited?**

**A:** Anyone who signs up can come! It is not mandatory ( purely voluntary ). Production, front office, 1st shift, 2nd shift, all are welcome!
